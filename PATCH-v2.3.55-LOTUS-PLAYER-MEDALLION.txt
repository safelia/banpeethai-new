BanPeeThai v2.3.55 — Lotus Player Medallion

Visual changes
- Replaced the old circular player token treatment with a clean Thai lotus medallion frame: double gold rim, centered avatar, player name, key progress, and life-heart row.
- Removed the previous crossed/radial decoration from the player frame; decorative lotus details sit above and below the circle instead of crossing the content.
- The local player's own turn (.my-turn) gets a bright pulsing crimson rim, red halo, and brighter avatar.
- Other players retain a subdued gold/black frame. A bot or remote player's active turn does not trigger the local red highlight.
- Added compact mobile sizing and reduced-motion fallback.
